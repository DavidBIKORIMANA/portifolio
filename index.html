CARE System — Electronic Medical Records

An EMR running live across 40+ health centers, at 99%+ uptime.

CARE is a native PHP electronic medical record system — built with PHP, JavaScript, jQuery, HTML, CSS, and MySQL — to store and retrieve patient data reliably across dozens of health centers at once. Uptime at that scale isn't a demo metric — it's proactive monitoring, rapid incident response, and infrastructure decisions made to hold under real clinical load.

I own it end to end: full-stack development, API integrations, and the ongoing IT maintenance that keeps it live in production — including the official maintenance contracts and reports for centers such as SIMBI and Rusatira, drafted in Kinyarwanda and English through DOCX XML manipulation and Node.js generation.
